HELP & TROUBLESHOOTING

How can we help?

A direct path from discovery to an authorized connection.

01

My TV does not appear

  1. Wake your TV and connect both devices to a local network that permits device-to-device traffic. Guest or hotel Wi-Fi may isolate devices.
  2. On iPhone, check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network and allow Remote Deck TV. Return and tap Scan again.
  3. Where supported, find your own TV's IP in its Network settings and choose Connect a TV for manual setup.

Never try to discover or control neighbors' TVs without permission.

02

My TV appears, but commands do not work

Discovery, pairing and actual command delivery are separate steps. Select the intended supported TV, then approve its prompt on the TV itself if one appears.

AirPlay, Cast, Roku and VIDAA advertisements may be identification-only. A discovered name does not guarantee Remote Deck can control that model. Use the manufacturer's official app for unsupported devices.

03

Connection stopped or changed

Confirm the TV is still accessible on the same permitted Wi-Fi. If its private IP changed, review the address before pairing again. Do not silently trust a different device with the same display name.

Demo controls are simulations and do not contact real TVs.

04

Forget a TV or revoke network access

In Remote Deck, open Devices → More options → Forget TV. You can also disable Local Network access in iPhone Settings. Some shared cryptographic identities may remain in iOS Keychain after deleting an individual TV or uninstalling the app.

Report an issue

Use our public GitHub support tracker to report a bug or ask a privacy question. Submitting a ticket requires a GitHub account, and posts may be public. Include only the intended TV's manufacturer/model, firmware, iOS and Remote Deck version, and reproduction steps.

Never post passwords, TV pairing codes, IP addresses, Keychain tokens, neighbors' device data or Apple Account information.
